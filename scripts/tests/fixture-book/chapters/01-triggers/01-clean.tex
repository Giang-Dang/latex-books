\section{A clean section}
\label{sec:ch01-clean}

\index{fixture!clean}%
Nothing in this section may produce a finding. It cites a real key with the
tie~\autocite{realkey}, quotes with \enquote{the right macro}, and reports a
measurement of 1.25 seconds that the research note records.

Masking is exercised here too. The span \code{it's a color--center "thing"}
holds a contraction, two American spellings, a dash ligature and a literal
quote, and every one of them sits inside the inline-code macro, so none may be
reported. If this section ever produces a finding, the masking broke.
