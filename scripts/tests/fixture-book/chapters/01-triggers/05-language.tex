\section{Language}
\label{sec:ch01-language}

It's a contraction in the author's voice, which is the one thing the
contraction check reports. The possessive on the second word is not one.

The color of the center was gray: three American spellings on one line.

The neighbor is the fourth, and the one the table was missing.

A displayed quotation is somebody else's words, so nothing below is reported
even though both checks would fire on it anywhere else. The expected list is
compared exactly, which is what makes this a test rather than a comment.

\begin{quote}
It's the neighbor's color, and it isn't ours.
\end{quote}
