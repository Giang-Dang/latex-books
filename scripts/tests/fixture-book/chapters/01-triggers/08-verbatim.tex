\section{Verbatim claims}
\label{sec:ch01-verbatim}

Here is what the tool printed, in full:

\begin{minted}{text}
this line was really captured
{"errors":[{"message":"no such field","line":14}]}
this line was never captured anywhere
\end{minted}

The first line is in the research note and must pass. The second is in the note
too, folded across two lines there and printed on one here, and must also pass:
a note that wraps a long capture for readability has still recorded it. Note
where the note folds, which is mid-token and not at a space, because that is
what makes the difference between removing whitespace and collapsing it. The
third line is in no note and is the finding.
