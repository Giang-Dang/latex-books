\section{Quoting}
\label{sec:ch01-quoting}

This line carries a literal "double quote" in prose, which is the whole of what
the quote check looks for.

A diaeresis is not a quotation mark. Philip M\"uller and Kurt G\"{o}del keep
their names in the bare and the braced form, and neither is a finding, because
strict Ascii mode leaves a book no other way to spell them.

A line break before one still is a finding.\\"like this"
