\section{Letters of other scripts}
\label{sec:ch01-letters}

Every line below must pass. These are letters, not punctuation, and a check
that rejects them is a check no book outside English can live with.

Vietnamese: Cuốn sách này viết về kiến trúc phần mềm hiện đại.
German: Groesse, and with the letter itself: Größe.
French: une reference, and with its accents: une référence.
Polish: Zażółć gęślą jaźń.
Greek: Το γράμμα άλφα.
