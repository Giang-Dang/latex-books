\section{Captured output}
\label{sec:ch01-captured}

Four em dashes, one per case, for Characters.AllowInCapturedListings. With the
setting absent every one of them is a finding. With the setting naming
minted:text, exactly one of them stops being one, and which one is the whole
behaviour: inside a named environment AND traceable to a research note.

Case 1, inside minted:text and traceable. This is the only forgivable one.

\begin{minted}{text}
The generated config keeps the directive—this time without its argument.
\end{minted}

Case 2, inside minted:text and traceable to nothing. Somebody typed it.

\begin{minted}{text}
An invented line of output nobody ever captured—typed straight into the draft.
\end{minted}

Case 3, traceable, but in an environment the setting does not name.

\begin{minted}{json}
The generated config keeps the directive—this time without its argument.
\end{minted}

Case 4, prose. Traceable word for word, and still not a listing.

The generated config keeps the directive—this time without its argument.
