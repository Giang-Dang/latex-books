# Tone: Social

**Use for:** LinkedIn and X posts, short public notes read once on a phone.

**Person and personality level:** `full`, but compressed. "I" for the author. No corporate "we"
unless the post goes out for an organization.

**Sentence rhythm:** short. The whole post under roughly 150 words. One claim, one concrete detail
that carries it, then stop. No closing paragraph.

**Terminology:** keep it and do not define it. The reader is in the field.

## Tells specific to this tone

- **Broetry.** Every sentence given its own paragraph to fake pacing. Write paragraphs.
- **Hook formulas.** "I was wrong.", "3 years ago, I...", "Nobody talks about this, but...",
  "Unpopular opinion:". Open with the claim itself.
- **§32 aphorism closers.** "X is the new Y", "X isn't a tool, it's a mindset". Say the specific
  thing the aphorism is gesturing at.
- **Engagement bait.** "Thoughts?", "Agree?", "Drop a comment below".
- **Hashtag stacks** and §18 emoji used as bullet markers.

## Before and after

**Before:**

> 🚀 I was wrong.
>
> For 3 years I thought optimizing code was the most important skill.
>
> It isn't.
>
> Measurement is the real currency of performance work. 💡
>
> Thoughts? #performance #softwareengineering

**After:** For three years I treated optimizing code as the main skill in performance work.
Measuring first matters more.
