\chapter{Data Without the N+1}
\label{ch:data-without-the-n-plus-1}

A hundred and forty-six lookups, answered from a list in memory, cost three
milliseconds. Chapter~\ref{ch:hotchocolate-quickly} measured that and admitted
it. Chapter~\ref{ch:the-life-of-a-request} read the code that ran them and found
that the engine had executed a hundred and forty-six resolvers to do it, one for
every question asked. What neither chapter could tell you is what the number was
worth, because a \code{List<T>} answers everything for free, so the count
measured nothing but itself.

This chapter gives the questions a price. Mosaic moves onto PostgreSQL through
Entity Framework Core, the same six domains behind the same six folders, and the
hundred and forty-six lookups become a hundred and forty-six statements. Then
DataLoaders bring that to three.

The resolver count does not move. It is a hundred and forty-six before and a
hundred and forty-six after, and holding those two numbers next to each other is
most of what this chapter has to teach. The engine still runs every resolver it
ran in chapter~\ref{ch:the-life-of-a-request}; what changes is what a resolver
does when it gets there. Nothing about the schema changes at all, which is the
property that makes this fix applicable one field at a time to a service already
in production.

Because the before and the after both matter, the companion repository carries
two tags for this chapter. \code{ch04-ef} is Mosaic on PostgreSQL with the naive
lookups intact, and \code{ch04} is the same service with DataLoaders and a paged,
filtered, sorted catalog field. Every number below is reproducible from one of
them. Internals are read from HotChocolate at tag 16.6.0, commit
\code{8fea46e}~\autocite{chillicream2026source}, as they were in the last
chapter.
