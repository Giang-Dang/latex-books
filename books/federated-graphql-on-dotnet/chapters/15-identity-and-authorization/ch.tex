\chapter{Identity and Authorization Across the Graph}
\label{ch:identity-and-authorization}

Fourteen chapters of this book have built a graph that answers every question
to everybody. Chapter~\ref{ch:hotchocolate-quickly} shipped a customer's email
address on a public field and said nothing about it, and nothing since has
changed that. The storefront is fine. The email address is not, and it has been
sitting there ever since.

So this chapter puts one rule on one field and follows what happens, which
turns out to be a long way. HotChocolate has two ways to protect a field and
they are unrelated to each other. One of them is enforced by the service and is
invisible to the router. The other is published to the router and is not
enforced by the service. A field carrying only the first is unreachable through
the router by anybody, including the person it was meant for, and the error the
client gets names neither authentication nor the reason.

That is the chapter. Underneath it are the two smaller questions any graph with
a token has to answer. Where the token is checked, given that there are now
eight processes that could check it and only one of them faces the client. And
what a refusal looks like, which turns out to have two shapes with two HTTP
statuses and two spellings of the same word, and a client that treats them as
one condition will get it wrong.

By the end Mosaic has a signing key, seven services that validate the same
tokens, one field that requires a scope, one field that requires a login, and a
script that mints a token so that a reader can see all of it move. It also has
a defect report it did not go looking for: the router's own explanation of why
it refused a field names the field correctly and the type as eight zero bytes.

The router is Cosmo 0.337.1, and its version has not moved since
chapter~\ref{ch:enter-the-router}; what this chapter adds to it is three blocks
of configuration. Its source is read at tag \code{router@0.337.1}, commit
\code{2f50ab8}.
The composer is wgc 0.129.7 over \code{@wundergraph/composition} 0.63.2. Every
line of library source is HotChocolate at tag \code{16.6.0}, commit
\code{8fea46e}.
