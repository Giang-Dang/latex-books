\chapter{Schema Design That Survives Change}
\label{ch:schema-design-that-survives-change}

Mosaic has been read-only for four chapters and has never once had to tell a
caller no. Nothing has been deprecated, because nothing has been replaced. No
identifier has meant anything outside the request that produced it. The schema
has been a description of what the service can do, which is the easy half; this
chapter is about the other half, where the schema is a promise about what it
will go on doing.

Three changes land here, and the point of the chapter is that they are not the
same kind of change. The \code{products} field is deprecated in favour of the
paged one chapter~\ref{ch:data-without-the-n-plus-1} added, and every client
that uses it keeps working. Identifiers stop being raw \code{Guid}s and become
opaque, globally unique values, which breaks every client holding a stored one.
\code{Product.reviews} stops being a list and becomes a connection, which breaks
every client that selects it. A chapter about surviving change that ships two
breakages is being honest about the ratio: some changes cannot be made
survivably, and the skill is knowing which, and what the survivable ones cost.

Along the way the schema acquires its first abstract types, its first mutation,
its first error model and its first subscription. Those arrive together because
they belong together. An error model needs something that can fail before it has
anything to describe, and an interface and a union turn out to be what the
description is made of.

The numbers that matter carry over unchanged. A hundred and forty-six resolvers
and three statements, which is where
chapter~\ref{ch:data-without-the-n-plus-1} left the catalog query, are still a
hundred and forty-six resolvers and three statements at the end of this one, and
the statement in the middle has become a window function without anybody asking
it to. Everything below is reproducible from tag \code{ch05} of the companion
repository. Internals are read from HotChocolate at tag 16.6.0, commit
\code{8fea46e}~\autocite{chillicream2026source}, as in the last two chapters.
