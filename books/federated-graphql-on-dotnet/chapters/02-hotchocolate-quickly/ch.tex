\chapter{HotChocolate, Quickly}
\label{ch:hotchocolate-quickly}

Chapter~\ref{ch:why-one-graph-is-never-enough} was an argument. This one is a
service. By the end of it Mosaic exists as running code on .NET~10, with six
domains inside one deployable, and you will have queried it from a browser and
from Postman.

The word \emph{quickly} in the title is a promise about the first section only.
HotChocolate will serve a schema after about six lines of setup, and I want to
get that out of the way early so the rest of the chapter can be spent on the
decisions that actually cost something later: which of the ways to describe a
type you commit to, where a field lives when four teams have a claim on the type
it hangs from, and what a development loop looks like when the schema is
generated rather than written.

I also want to end the chapter with something broken. Mosaic as this chapter
leaves it does a stupid amount of work to answer one query, and it does it on
purpose. Section~\ref{sec:ch02-still-wrong} counts exactly how much.
