\chapter{Inside HotChocolate}
\label{ch:inside-hotchocolate}

Chapter~\ref{ch:the-life-of-a-request} printed a list of thirteen middleware and
stopped at the last one on it, which is the line that hands a compiled operation
to the executor. Everything on the far side of that line is this chapter.

Four things were written down as unchecked at the time, which is the only reason
they are not mine to remember wrong: which validation rules survive a document
cache hit, what happens to compilation when a burst of identical requests
arrives at once, what the diagnostic event called \code{RunTask} actually
counts, and where the two authorization middleware
chapter~\ref{ch:identity-and-authorization} located in a startup log live in the
source. All four are paid here.

\index{HotChocolate!reading the executor}%
Reading an executor is not the same kind of work as the rest of this book. There
is no composer to disagree with and no router to configure. There is a source
tree, a debugger's worth of patience, and the discipline of checking that the
thing you are describing is the thing you ran. Every listing below names the
file it came out of, and the ones that are measurements say what produced them.

Three of the findings surprised me. The number this book has printed since
chapter~\ref{ch:the-life-of-a-request} counts something narrower than its name
suggests, and the event sitting next to it counts nothing at all on any request
Mosaic serves. A compiled operation is not finished when it goes into the cache:
it keeps compiling, under a lock, for as long as it lives there, and what it
compiles depends on what your traffic returns. And one line
chapter~\ref{ch:identity-and-authorization} added to three services, for reasons
that had nothing to do with caching, stopped those three skipping validation on
every repeat request they will ever serve.

That last one is the one I would want to know about, and it is invisible from
every artefact this book has learned to read: the schema does not change, so
nothing composed from it changes either. It took a control before I believed it.
I added the same line to a service with nothing guarded in it at all, and
watched the same thing happen there.

Every line of library source here is HotChocolate at tag \code{16.6.0}, commit
\code{8fea46e}~\autocite{chillicream2026source}, cloned locally rather than read
a page at a time. The companion repository is at tag \code{ch16}, at which no
Mosaic service changes by a line: what the tag adds is
\code{samples/executor-internals}, five cases that assert the facts this chapter
prints, and two new steps in each verification script.
