\chapter{The Life of a Request}
\label{ch:the-life-of-a-request}

Chapter~\ref{ch:hotchocolate-quickly} ended with a number and a confession. One
query, a hundred and forty-six lookups, and a counter that took two attempts
because a resolver's injected services do not come from the scope I assumed they
did. I fixed the counter, said the fix was worth more than the number, and moved
on. This chapter goes back and reads the code that made me wrong.

Between an HTTP request arriving and the first line of your resolver running,
HotChocolate does thirteen things. Some of them you can skip, one of them is not
where you think it is, and two of them are caches that make the second identical
request cost meaningfully less than the first. None of this is exotic: it is a
list of middleware, in a fixed order, that you can print at startup.

That printing matters more than it sounds. Everything measured in this chapter
came out of the service itself rather than out of a documentation page, and
section~\ref{sec:ch03-instrumenting} shows the code that produced it.
Where the source and my expectations disagreed, the source won and I have said
so. Where the first measurement was wrong, I have said that too, because the way
it was wrong is the useful part.

This is also the first of the chapters that reads HotChocolate's own code. When
one of those walks makes a claim, it names the tag it was read against. For this
chapter that is 16.6.0 throughout, which resolves to commit
\code{8fea46e}~\autocite{chillicream2026source}, and the companion repository is
at tag \code{ch03}.
