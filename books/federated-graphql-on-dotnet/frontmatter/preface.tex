This book is for .NET developers who know GraphQL basics and want to build,
run, and deeply understand federated graphs: HotChocolate subgraphs composed,
routed, and operated with WunderGraph Cosmo.

\section*{Version baseline}

Written against HotChocolate 16.6, HotChocolate.ApolloFederation 16.6,
WunderGraph Cosmo (router and wgc CLI, current as of August 2026), ChilliCream
Fusion 16.5 (chapter 27), the Apollo Federation specification v2.15, and
.NET 10 LTS. The living baseline, with verification dates, is tracked in the
book's SPEC.md.
