\chapter{The N+1 the Router Creates}
\label{ch:router-n-plus-1}

A reference resolver is handed a list and called once for every entry in it.
Everything in this chapter follows from that sentence, and none of it is
visible outside the service where it happens.

The cost lands on whichever subgraph the router asks, and one of them in this
graph has been paying it since chapter~\ref{ch:first-subgraph}, with a comment
in the code saying so and naming this chapter as the place it gets fixed.
