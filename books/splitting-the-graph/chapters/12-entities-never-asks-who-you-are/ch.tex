\chapter{\code{\_entities} Never Asks Who You Are}
\label{ch:entities-authorization}

A guard on a root field guards that root field, and the entity route reaches
the same rows without ever going near it.

There are three doors into a subgraph's data and the attribute you reach for
covers one of them. The other two are listed in the schema any caller can
introspect, which is how somebody finds them without knowing your code.
