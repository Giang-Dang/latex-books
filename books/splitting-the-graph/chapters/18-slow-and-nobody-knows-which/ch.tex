\chapter{Slow, and Nobody Knows Which Subgraph}
\label{ch:blame-routing}

The router gives every request one trace id and sends it to every subgraph it
asks, and nobody can say which subgraph was slow, or why the one the router names
failed, because no service writes that id down and the router never hands it to the person
reporting the problem.
