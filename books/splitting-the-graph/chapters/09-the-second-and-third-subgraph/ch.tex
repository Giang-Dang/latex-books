\chapter{The Second and Third Subgraph}
\label{ch:second-third-subgraph}

A seam is a type two services both name and one of them owns. This chapter
cuts two of them, and they are different shapes: the Speakers service takes a
whole type away from Sessions, and the Ratings service adds fields to a type it
will never own a row of.

What that costs is a stub on the side giving something up, a reference resolver
on the side taking it, and one more fetch in every plan that crosses. What it
buys is the graph the rest of the book uses.
