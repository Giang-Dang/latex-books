\chapter{The Page You Cannot Ask For}
\label{ch:cross-seam-paging}

A page is cut by the service that owns the list, out of values that service
holds, so a page sorted by a score another service owns is a page this graph
cannot ask for.

Chapter~\ref{ch:federation-model} promised this bill when it drew the seam
between Sessions and Ratings, and the first request below is the bill
arriving.
