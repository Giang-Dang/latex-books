\begin{problemsummary}
{A paged list sorted or filtered by a field another service contributes comes
back in the list owner's own order, at HTTP 200 with no error. Ask
\code{sessions} for the first row by \code{averageScore} and you get the first
row of the schedule with its score attached, and nothing in the schema lets
you ask for anything else.}
{The service that owns the list cuts the page before any entity fetch runs,
out of the values it holds, and a later fetch can only decorate the rows the
cut kept. \code{@requires}, \code{@provides}, \code{@shareable} and
\code{@listSize} each describe something else, and none of them hands the
list owner a value before its resolver runs.}
{Give the search surface to a service that holds every value it filters and
sorts by. Here a fourth subgraph, Search, keeps a complete projection, filters
and cuts the page in one statement, and hands Session keys to Sessions, which
hydrates them in one batch. What that service now owns is the projection's
completeness, its freshness and its ordering, and the schema should say what
each of those promises.}
\end{problemsummary}
