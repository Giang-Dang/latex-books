\chapter{Your First Subgraph}
\label{ch:first-subgraph}

Becoming a subgraph adds a second way into every object your service owns, and
it is not the one you spent chapter~\ref{ch:schema-design} designing.

Three edits do it. A package, one line in \code{Program.cs}, and two attributes
per entity. What comes back is a schema with fields you did not write, a route
that reaches your rows without going through any field a client can see, and a
resolver that is handed less than every other resolver in this book and asked
for the same thing.
